About

Discipline
over ideas.

Most ideas never ship. We decided to flip that. Every month, we pick one problem, build the most focused solution possible, and put it in people's hands — no waiting for perfect, no endless roadmaps.

Yissue is a studio built on the belief that momentum compounds. Twelve apps a year. Some will stick. Some won't. All of them teach us something.

How it works

Thirty days, one ship.

01
Week 1 — Define
One problem. One audience. One clear outcome. We research, validate, and lock the scope before writing a single line of code.
02
Week 2 — Design & Build
Design and development run in parallel. We prototype fast, cut what doesn't belong, and keep only what earns its place.
03
Week 3 — Polish
Typography, motion, edge cases. The layer most teams skip entirely. We treat it as the product itself.
04
Week 4 — Ship & Learn
Live. Real users. Real data. We document what we learn and carry every lesson into the next month's build.
